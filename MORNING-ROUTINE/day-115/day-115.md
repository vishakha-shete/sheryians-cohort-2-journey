# 🌱 Day 115 — Documenting My Learning Journey

## 🤝 Morning — Talking About the Walk-In Opportunity

Today wasn't a very productive study day, but I did spend some time thinking seriously about my job search.

I communicated with a friend and asked her if she could come with me for a **walk-in interview in Nagpur**.

She said yes and suggested that she could settle there after September. The idea is that we could go to Nagpur for the walk-in, attend the interview, and hopefully spend some time there studying and focusing on our careers.

For a moment, this gave me some hope that maybe I can create a better environment for myself and focus more seriously on getting a job.

## 📞 Another Unexpected Responsibility

But today morning, I received a call asking me to go after **Mahalakshmi** to take care of and work for Shalu Aai.

And honestly, I don't know what to do anymore.

I had just started thinking about going for the Nagpur walk-in, and now another responsibility has come up.

My mind immediately started asking:

> *How will I prepare for interviews?*
> *When will I complete my cohort?*
> *How will I manage everything?*
> *How will I travel to Nagpur and come back in one day?*
> *What if I have to leave again?*

I'm taking so much tension about everything at once.

---

## 😔 Feeling Stuck

It makes me feel like I'm constantly starting and stopping.
